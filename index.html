☁️ About iWeather

Developed by: Harsh Kumar Soni

Purpose: This app helps users find real-time weather details like temperature, wind speed, and humidity for any city in the world.

Tech Stack: HTML, Bootstrap, CSS, JavaScript & OpenWeatherMap API.

Key Features: Geolocation Tracking, QR Code Generation & Web Sharing, Live Unit Conversion (°C/°F).

© 2026 All rights reserved.

📖 How to Use?

  • Step 1: Click on the Search Box at the top.
  • Step 2: Enter your city name (e.g., Mumbai, Delhi, London).
  • Step 3: Click the Search button.
  • Result: You will see Temperature, Humidity, and Wind details etc instantly!
  • Note 1: You can also use the "Use My Location" feature to instantly get the weather details of your current city.
  • Note 2: Also, you can use the "Switch to °F" feature to change the temperature unit from Celsius to Fahrenheit and vice versa.
  • Note 3: You can also scan the "QR Code" with your phone to instantly view the weather details on your mobile device.
Tip: For accurate results, add country code (e.g., 'Delhi, IN').
